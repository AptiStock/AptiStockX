# Support

For AptiStockX support, email:

- support@aptistock.com

Helpful details to include:

- AptiStockX version
- Operating system and version
- Installer type, such as DMG, EXE, AppImage, DEB, or Homebrew
- A short description of the problem
- Screenshots or exact error messages when relevant

For license, activation, payment, or refund help, contact support from the purchase or activation email address when possible.
